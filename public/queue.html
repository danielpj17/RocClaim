    How to start

    • Each person needs their own Chrome profile, signed in to their own BYU account, with the extension loaded. Their ROC pass, their ticket.
    • In each profile: open the game page, open the side panel, tick Take turns, type that person's name, press Watch this tab.
    • The first one starts the queue with its stop time. Everyone else joins it and waits; only the person who is up searches.
    • Someone counts as done only once their order is placed. If a seat's 10-minute hold runs out, they keep their turn.
